Overview
Purpose and Scope
This document specifies the functional requirements for the Case Management System (CMS) component of the SDC Island Integrated Operations Centre platform, covering 
Incident Management
Fault Reporting
Task Management
No-Objection Permit
Events Master List
E-Diary
Distribution Group Management
Broadcast and Notification
CMS Dashboard
System Configuration and Administration
CMS Modules


The CMS is a web browser-based platform. All modules are subject to role-based access control; the specific modules, tabs, and functions visible to a given user upon login are determined by their assigned role. The following modules are covered in this document.
Module
Description
Incident Management
Creation, assignment, tracking, and closure of incident records from all reporting sources across the island
Fault Reporting
Creation, automatic triage, routing, and closure of fault records, with rule-based assignment to the IFM contractor and CMMS ticket creation via API where required
Creation, tracking and closure of fault records. Creation of CMMS tickets via API.
Task Management
Creation, assignment and tracking of operational tasks directed to Responders or SDC staff with CMS accounts within a Case
Examples: welfare check, escort, item collection, access arrangements
No-Objection Permit
Permit application, document submission, works-area mapping, review and approval, permit lifecycle management
Events Master List
Creation, import and management of events, including list and calendar views
e-Diary
Creation and retrieval of operational diary entries that cannot be amended or deleted after submission, with support for creating or linking Incidents, Tasks and events
Distribution Group Management
Creation and maintenance of recipient groups used for broadcasts and notifications
Broadcast and Notification
Structured framework for disseminating incident information to defined distribution groups.
Crisis Recall
Recall notification function for selected group of SDC stakeholders governed by the crisis-level classification governing escalation and recall
CMS Dashboard
Consolidated operational and calendar event view of active cases, incidents, faults, and tasks, with a filterable 2D map of island-wide activity
Central Rostering
Proposed module for managing duty rosters and surfacing assigned response team, Duty Manager and other on-duty personnel in CMS and UCS operational views
System Configuration
Administration of user accounts, role assignments, classification taxonomies, location hierarchy, routing matrices, distribution groups, and audit settings
Authentication and Access Control
Authentication Method
SDC users shall authenticate through Whole-of Government Single Sign-ON (WOG SSO). WOG SSO authentication alone shall not grant access to the CMS. The CMS shall verify that the authenticated user has an active, authorised CMS account and an assigned system role. Users who do not meet these conditions shall be denied access and directed to contact the System Administrator.
Non-SDC users shall authentication using a CMS username and password, with 2-factor authentication.
CMS Dashboard
The CMS Dashboard shall provide a consolidated operational view for Controllers, Duty Officers, and Duty Managers.
It is distinct from the UCS Dashboard in scope and purpose: the UCS Dashboard presents live-state summary counts and high-level situational awareness, while the CMS Dashboard provides access to both current and historical records with line-item detail views.
Landing Page and Navigation
Upon successful authentication, users are presented with the CMS landing page. Navigation items visible to a given user are filtered by their assigned role.
Navigation Item
Description
Dashboard
Consolidated operational view of active cases, incidents, faults, tasks, events, no-objection permits, and 2D island map
Case Management
Case log list view and case detail view, with access to linked incidents, faults, tasks and e-diary entries
Incident Management
Incident log list view, incident creation and management, and incident detail view
Fault Reporting
Fault log list view, fault creation and management, and fault detail view

Task log list view, task creation and assignment, and task default view

2D Island Map
The CMS Dashboard shall include an embedded 2D map of Sentosa Island leveraging on OneMap and its available API. The map will provide a consolidated spatial view of active operational activity across the island and is accessible to all users with Dashboard access.
Map Layer
Description
Incidents
Drop-pins for active incident records, derived from the text-entered location data on each incident log. Coordinates are resolved from the entered address, road name, building, or postal code via the OneMap geocoding API
For incidents at locations without a standard address (e.g. beach areas, open outdoor spaces), coordinates are taken from the manually placed pin drop on the incident record.
Faults
Drop-pins for active fault records, resolved by geocoding
Active Works
Boundary overlays for approved and active NOP works areas. Works boundaries drawn by NOP applicants during their application are automatically displayed on this map upon permit approval, providing Controllers with a live spatial overview of all ongoing permitted works on the island
Events
Boundary overlays for upcoming or ongoing events from the Events Master List, based on the Event Location and Event area mapped within the selected location hierarchy
For locations with defined physical boundaries, such as named buildings and venues, the system shall associate a text-entered location with the corresponding pinned coordinate.
For road-based locations, which cannot be geofenced, coordinate resolution relies on the geocoding result returned by OneMap for the entered road name or common name.
Island events at fixed venue sites, such as pavilions, event grounds, and beaches, shall be represented on the map as drop-pin icons.
Geographical Coverage
The map shall support plotting of records across Sentosa Island, and all named off-island SDC operational locations, including:
Mount Faber Leisure Group premises on Mt Faber and Harbourfront Tower
SDC-operated berths at Harbourfront Bus Interchange
VivoCity, nearby MRT stations and bus stops, and other transport nodes in the immediate vicinity
Designated crisis locations
Road routes to and from Sentosa Gateway, including all road names on these routes
Map Display Behaviour
Behaviour
Description
Pin Clustering 
When multiple pins of the same layer are in proximity at the current zoom level, they are aggregated into a numbered cluster marker showing the record count
Zoom to Expand
Clicking a cluster marker zooms the map to the cluster area, expanding it to reveal individual pins
Pin Interaction
Clicking an individual pin surfaces a summary panel showing the record type, ID, and current status, with a link to the full record in the CMS

Integration Dependency and Data Accuracy
Map functionality is dependent on the availability of the OneMap API, a government geospatial service maintained by SLA. SJ will be responsible for maintaining the API integration code in line with any changes to OneMap API versioning, endpoints, or authentication specifications. The accuracy and completeness of OneMap's underlying geospatial data is outside the development team's remit.
The spatial accuracy of plotted records is dependent on OneMap geocoding data, which may not reflect recent construction or changes to the built environment. For locations not yet reflected in OneMap, pin placement relies on the coordinate data in the CMS location taxonomy, which is maintained by the System Administrator via the System Configuration module.
Integration with Unified Command System
The CMS and the Unified Command System operate as complementary but functionally distinct platforms.
While the CMS is the system of record for all operational case data, the UCS is the primary command interface, surfacing a real-time operational overview to Controllers and the Duty Officer.
Live Metric Feed to UCS Dashboard
The CMS shall supply real-time summary counts to the UCS Dashboard, including the number of active cases, open incidents, open faults, active tasks.
Summary counts shall update in real time as records are created, updated or closed in the CMS. 

Embedded CMS Functions within UCS
CMS functions that are frequently used or operationally time-critical shall be surfaced as embedded panels or inline actions within the UCS, without requiring the user to navigate away from the UCS interface.
Actions performed through embedded UCS panels shall create, update, or retrieve records directly in the CMS. The UCS does not store or duplicate CMS data.
The embedded functions shall operate under the same role-based access controls as the full CMS.
Controllers shall be able to perform create, read and update operations for the following record types directly from within the UCS:
Record Type
Embedded Operations
Incidents
Create, view and update incident records. Assign responders and update incident status
Acknowledge VA triggers to create Incident records.
Tasks
Create, view, update and reassign task records
CMMS Faults
Create and view fault records, view CMMS ticket reference
e-Diary
Create, view, and amend e-Diary entries
No-Objection Permit
Actionable items involving IOH within NOP workflow
Broadcasts
Create, view, and amend draft Broadcasts. Dispatch authorised Broadcasts using CMS Broadcast templates, recipient groups and delivery-status records
Crisis Recall
View, initiate and confirm Crisis Recall for configured recall groups, including recall dispatch and acknowledgement status where supported by the approved messaging service
Last 10 Actions List
Displays the latest 10 operational actions performed by Controllers in the CMS.
Embedded operations shall be fully completable from within UCS, such as form input, validation and save shall be performable without the Controller navigating away to the CMS.
The embedded integration shall function as a working form and data interface, not a link or redirect to the CMS. Implementation method (inline panel, pop-up form, embedded component) is subject to technical design.
Navigation from UCS to CMS 
The UCS and CMS are complementary interfaces. CMS functions that are frequently used or operationally time-critical are surfaces as embedded panels or inline actions within the UCS. All remaining CMS functions are accessible from the UCS via direct navigation.
Where a function is not available as an embedded UCS action, selecting a live metric, summary item, or record reference in the UCS shall direct the user to the corresponding CMS module page.
Line-item detail, full record editing, case management, and reporting functions are handled within the CMS and are not replicated in the UCS.
The presentation of CMS data within the UCS, including widget layout, record list columns, display depth and filtering options will be defined in the UCS functional requirements document. A UCS record widget may surface a live list of active records with key fields, such as record ID, status, type and created at, alongside inline quick actions, for embedded operations.
Incident Creation from UCS
A Controller shall be able to initiate a new Incident record directly from the UCS without navigating to the CMS.
Incident creation from the UCS shall be available as an inline action, for example in response to a Video Analytics alert, a system-generated prompt, or a patrol report received in the UCS.
The Incident record shall be created and stored in the CMS. The UCS surfaces the creation interface only; it does not own the record.
Where a Video Analytics alert or system prompt is used as the trigger, the relevant alert reference shall be pre-populated into the Incident record where applicable.
Upon successful creation, the Controller shall be able to continue monitoring the UCS or navigate directly to the full Incident record in the CMS.
External Weather Alert Forwarding
Where weather forecast or weather alert information is surfaces on the UCS, authorised users may initiate an advisory broadcast from the relevant alert
The advisory broadcast uses configured CMS broadcast templates, recipient groups and delivery channels.
Last 10 Actions from CMS to UCS Widget
The widget shall display the latest 10 operational actions performed by the Controllers in the CMS
Each entry shall show the Controller, action, affected record, and date and time
Actions include record creation, assignment, status changes and closure of operational records
Broadcast Integration with UCS
Broadcast actions surfaced on the UCS use CMS Broadcast records, templates, recipient groups and delivery-status information.
Authorised users may create, review, amend and dispatch Broadcasts drafts from the UCS.
Where an Incident has Broadcast draft or dispatched Broadcast, the UCS displays an indicator on the Incident record, of which selecting it would open the related draft or dispatched Broadcast details
The CMS remains the system of record for Broadcast records and dispatch history.
Crisis Recall with UCS
The UCS shall surface Crisis Recall functions for authorised users during crisis operations.
When an Incident classification triggers Crisis Recall, the UCS shall display the queued recall action, applicable Recall Group and message status for user review.
The Duty Manager shall be able to review and confirm Crisis Recall dispatch from their UCS terminal
The UCS shall display recall dispatch status where supported by the approved messaging service.

Record Data Retention
When a user account is deactivated or removed, their associated records should remain accessible to users with appropriate role permissions, with the original creator's identity preserved on each record.
Stakeholder Incident Record Access
Upon creation of an Incident and confirmation of Incident Type, CMS accounts of SDC stakeholders associated with the relevant incident type will be granted view-only access to the Incident Record for incident visibility and live updates of the incident log.

Case Data Model
The Case is the top-level record in the CMS. All operational activity is anchored to a Case.
Entity Structure and Relationships
For the purpose of the CMS, an operational event is any occurrence requiring official action by a Controller or above, including security incidents, fault reports, directed tasks, and e-Diary log entries. 
A Case is created for each operational event, and the sub-record that triggers the Case carries the operational detail. The CMS data model is organised around the Case as a lean operational record container. The Case holds an identifier, a status, and references to its associated sub-records.
A Case may be created independently of any child record.
A Case may contain zero or one Incident record.
A Case may contain zero or more Task records.
A Case may reference zero or more CMMS Ticket IDs. These identifiers are returned by the IFM CMMS upon ticket creation and stored as reference attributes of the Case. The ticket lifecycle is owned entirely by the IFM CMMS.
An Incident, Task, or CMMS Ticket reference cannot exist in the CMS without an associated Case.
Where a Case does not exist at the time of Incident or Task creation, the system shall auto-create a Case before creating the child record.
 e-Diary records are linked to a Case. A Case is auto-created on creation of an e-Diary entry if no existing Case context is present. An e-Diary entry does not trigger a ground response, work order, or broadcast, it is a log record only.
Events are managed as Events Master List records. They may be created from or linked to e-Diary entries for traceability, but they are not Case sub-records and do not affect status or automated Case closure.

Incident Ageing Alerts
Incident Ageing Alerts support Duty Manager and Controller review of long-running active Incidents.
Where an Incident remains in an active status for 12 consecutive days from the date of creation, an advance notification is generated to the assigned Controller and Duty Manager, prompting review of the Incident status.
Where an Incident remains in an active status for 14 consecutive days from the date of creation, a further notification is generated to the assigned Controller and Duty Manager, requiring action toward closure.
A daily Incident Ageing summary is generated for Duty Manager review 30 minutes after shift start.
The summary lists active Incidents that have reached the 12-day or 14-day ageing threshold, including Incident ID, Incident Type, assigned Controller, current status and days open.

Incident Logging
The Incident Log section is a dedicated segment within the incident record. section. It is accessible to the assigned Responder and Controllers and is used to log the actions taken during the ground response.

Duplicate Incident Detection and Handling
Multiple reports of the same incident may be received concurrently from different sources, e.g. several guests calling the 1800 hotline about the same event within a short period.
The system shall flag the Controller when a potential duplicate incident is detected during manual creation, based on matching criteria including date, time, incident type, and sub-type.
The Controller retains the option to proceed with creating a new record, override the duplicate flag, or link the new record to an existing incident
Where a record is confirmed as a duplicate, it shall be marked as a linked duplicate and not treated as an independent active Incident
Linked duplicates shall not generate independent response workflows or separate broadcast triggers
Closing the Master Incident shall automatically close all linked duplicate records. 
Incident Record Save and Concurrent Editing
Save Mechanism
The system shall auto-save all form input at regular intervals while an incident record is being edited, to minimise data loss in the event of session timeout or interruption.
A manual save action shall also be available to the user at any point during editing.
A record in an unsaved state shall display a visible indicator to the user.
Unsaved changes shall not be discarded without user confirmation.
The auto-save interval shall be configurable by the System Administrator.
Concurrent Editing
Where two Controllers access and edit the same incident record simultaneously, the system shall manage the conflict to prevent one Controller's saved input from silently overwriting the others without notification. 
Scenario
Required Behaviour
Second user opens a record already being edited
The system shall notify the second user that the record is currently being edited by another user
Both users save changes to the same record
The system shall not silently discard either user's input. A conflict resolution mechanism shall be applied to surface the discrepancy and prompt resolution
Linked Fault Creation
Where an incident reveals an underlying infrastructure defect, a Fault Report may be created directly from the incident record.
A Controller may raise a fault record from within an active Incident where physical defect is identified as part of or resulting from the incident.
Fault creation from an Incident record shall initiate the CMMS ticket creation workflow
The fault record shall be linked to the originating incident and shall be visible from both the incident and fault detail views.
The incident and fault are managed independently once created. Either may be closed before the other.
Incident may be closed after the immediate situation is resolved while the fault record remains open as the infrastructure repair and investigation continue.
False Alarm Handling
Where an incoming report or VA alert is reviewed by the Controller and determined to require no action, the Controller may log the record and mark it as a false alarm.
A false alarm record, including the identity of the closing user, shall be retained in the system as a closed record for audit and reporting purposes.
Incident Closure and Reopening
Incident closure requires Duty Manager or designated in-charge approval, except for false alarm closures
A Controller shall submit an Incident for closure by transitioning it to Pending Endorsement status
The Duty Manager shall review the record and either approve closure or return it with comments.
Upon approval, the Incident status changes to Closed and the Case closure process is triggered if all linked records are resolve.
To reopen a closed incident, a System Administrator must revert the status from Closed to the appropriate active status.
Once the Incident status is updated to Closed, the system shall generate a closure broadcast prompt for the Controller, where a closure broadcast is required under the configured broadcast rules. 
Broadcasting Triggers
Broadcast specifications including delivery channels, templates, recipient group management, crisis level classification, and the routing matrix are defined in Section 10: Broadcast and Notification Framework.
Closure Broadcast
The system shall automatically trigger a closure broadcast notification to the Controller when the Duty Manager or designated In-charge approves incident closure.
The Controller shall review the closure broadcast content, confirm the recipients, and dispatch the broadcast. One closure broadcast shall be issued per incident where closure broadcast is required.
The Incident record shall display the closure broadcast status and linked Broadcast ID once a closure broadcast has been dispatched
End-of-Day Interim Broadcast
For incidents that remain active at the end of the operational day, the system shall flag open incidents for inclusion in the end-of-day batch review queue.
The Duty Manager reviews and approves dispatch for each flagged incident

Task Management Module
Tasks are non-FM operational activities assigned to SDC staff for ground response or follow-up actions that do not constitute a formal FM work order.
Tasks are created by Controllers and are always linked to a Case.
A Task may exist within a Case independently of an Incident, or alongside an Incident where parallel ground activity is required.
Tasks are distinct from the Responder Activity log within an Incident record, which captures actions taken by an assigned Ranger during the incident response.
Tasks are distinct from CMMS tickets, which are FM work orders managed externally.
Task Creation
A Controller may create a Task at any point during the lifecycle of a Case. The system shall allow multiple Tasks to be created under the same Case. 
Requirements
Tasks are created by Controllers and higher roles (Duty Manager, Duty Officer, System Administrator) and are always linked to Case
A Task is linked to a Case and is not required to be associated with an Incident. 
Tasks may be created for any operational ground activity, including standalone requests such as item collection, escort, or access arrangements, as well as activities arising from or running in parallel with an active Incident.
Tasks can be set as a recurring event and may be assigned to one Assignee or a group.
For recurring Tasks, the recurrence schedule remains the template for creating future Task occurrences. Each generated Task is linked to its own Case and managed independently.

Task assignment groups may be configured by the System Administrator, to allow for assignment of Tasks to a group of users.

e-Diary Module
The e-Diary module is a module for recording events and interactions that fall under IOH responsibility but do not meet the threshold for a formal incident, fault, or task. The module digitises existing hardcopy occurrence logbooks currently maintained by the IOH Controllers, providing a searchable and filterable electronic record in place of the physical diary.
e-Diary records do not trigger a ground response, work order, or broadcast, and are not linked to the Case structure.
Common use cases include verbal interactions with members of the public, VIP and dignitary visit notifications, exercises, drills, and information dissemination activities.







































E-Diary Creation
The e-Diary module is primarily intended for use by Controllers. Duty Officers, Duty Managers, and System Administrators may also create e-Diary entries. All other CMS roles have no access to the e-Diary.
Creation requires no approval or assignment workflow.
e-Diary records can be edited even after submission but not deleted except by the System Administrator.
Requirements
The e-Diary is a sub-record under the Case structure. Where no Case exists at the time of creation, the CMS shall auto-create a Case.
Where an e-Diary entry escalates to require a formal response, a new Incident shall be created within the same Case. The e-Diary entry is retained as a journal record.
Entries are mutable, a Controller may amend an entry after submission. All amendments are tracked and timestamped.
File and image attachments are supported

Crisis Management and Emergency Recall
Crisis Recall is distinct from the broadcasts described in the previous section. While Broadcasts disseminate incident information to external recipients via email. Crisis Recall is the activation of named stakeholder groups to the command centre, triggered by an incident classified at Level 4+ or above, and delivered via selected communication medium.
Crisis Level Classification and Routing
Each incident shall be assigned a crisis level from 1 to 5 that determines the broadcast type, delivery channel, and recipient group as follows:

Level 1 is the most severe; Level 5 is the least severe (occurrence or false alarm).
Crisis level may be influenced by dynamic factors including media or press presence, reputational impact, and number of persons involved.
Crisis Recall
Crisis Recall shall be triggered when an incident is classified at Level 4+ or above.
Crisis Recall is semi-automated. An authorised user (Duty Manager) must reivew and confirm the recall before dispatch
At Level 4+, the specific relevant stakeholder group shall be recalled. At Level 3 and below, a broader set of SDC stakeholders shall be recalled.

For incidents at Level 4+ and above, the system shall generate and queue a crisis recall SMS based on the incident classification and configured recipient group.

Recall Groups
Recall groups are named collections of recipients used for Crisis Recall dispatch
The System Administrator can create, edit and deactivate Recall Groups
Each Recall Group includes the group name, description, members, contact details, membership status and applicable crisis recall routing rules
Recall Groups have similar group-management features as Broadcast distribution groups but are separate from them.
During crisis operations, an OR Analyst assigned to recall group operations can manage the operational recall group and its members for the active crisis, including adding, removing or updating members as required for crisis response.
